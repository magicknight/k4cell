Three ways to begin · 三种合作入口

  • Research patronage · 个人科研赞助

    For individuals who want more independent time spent on foundational theory, derivations, papers and prediction design. No scientific influence, financial return, equity or token right is implied.

    面向希望支持基础理论推导、专题论文撰写与先验预测设计的个人赞助者。赞助不附带科研裁决权、财务回报、股权或任何代币权利。

  • Institutional sponsorship · 机构与基金资助

    For foundations, institutes and research programmes. Every engagement starts with a written scope, deliverables, publication policy and conflict disclosure.

    面向科研基金会、研究机构与学术资助项目。每项合作均以书面形式明确研究范围、阶段产出、开放发表政策及利益冲突披露。

  • Strategic investment conversation · 战略投资讨论

    For partners interested in durable research infrastructure, public communication and a future organisational vehicle around the programme. This is an invitation to talk, not an offer of securities or tokens.

    面向关注长期科研基础设施、公共学术传播及未来科研载体的合作方。此项仅为初步交流邀请，并非任何证券或代币要约。

What new support unlocks · 新增科研支持将重点推进的方向

  • Founder research time for the open physical-realisation bridges.
  • New papers, open publication costs and professional scientific editing.
  • Reproducible computation, frozen evidence packages and independent review.
  • Founder-signed preregistered predictions before future data releases.
  • 支持作者投入专职时间攻关尚未闭合的物理实现接口，推进新专题论文发表、开放获取与科学润色、可复现计算数据包与独立核查，并在未来实验数据公布前签署可验证的先验预注册预测。

Roadmap · 推进路线图

  1. NOW · 2026

    Publish the next papers, develop source-native prediction candidates, improve the monograph and keep one sustained public narrative alive.
    推进下一批专题论文发表，梳理原生先验预测候选，持续修订完善专著，并维护严谨统一的公开学术叙事。

  2. FIRST CAPITAL

    Move from contact-only conversations to the first transparent funding relationship: written scope, disclosed conflicts and dedicated research runway. No token rights.
    由当前的邮件初步沟通迈向首笔公开透明的研究资助合作：确立书面研究范围、利益冲突披露及专职科研经费保障；全过程不附带任何代币权利。

  3. PREDICTION

    Sign the first prediction before its target data release, freeze the analysis path, and compare publicly when the experiment reports.
    在目标实验数据正式发布前完成首项可检验预测的签署与预注册，提前冻结分析路径，待实验结果公布后进行公开对照检验。

  4. INSTITUTION

    Build a durable home for the programme: a sponsored fellowship, foundation, lab or equivalent vehicle able to support multi-year work.
    为该研究计划建设可持续的制度化载体——例如资助席位、研究基金会、独立实验室或可承载多年期基础研究的同等实体机构。

  5. K4V · SEPARATE GATE

    Consider a K4V launch only after separate legal, governance, security and operational gates are published and Founder-signed. There is no launch date today.
    仅在独立的法律合规、治理机制、安全审计与运维门槛全部公开发布并经作者正式签署确认之后，方才评估 K4V 发行事宜；目前不存在任何发行时间表。
