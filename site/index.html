K4 / 01

Start with the object.

Four points, each connected to every other point, form the complete graph K4 with a three-state quantum switch per site (3⁴ = 81 basis states). The pigeonhole principle forces every classical colouring to clash on at least one link, driving a ninefold frustrated quantum ground level. The real test is separating closed finite-graph theorems from open physical bridges.

Check formulas, numbers, and open bridges separately

The full research page separates closed finite-system theorems, conditional derivations resting on named open interfaces (E3–E11), and author-reported Lean 4 counts whose source is not yet public. A failed test updates the claims it constrains rather than validating unrelated parts.
