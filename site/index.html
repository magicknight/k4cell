K4 / 01

Start with the object.

Four points, each connected to every other point, make the complete graph K4. The framework asks whether larger physical structures can follow from a finite local starting point. Numerical agreement matters, but so do the derivations and the conditions connecting a finite system to the physical world.

Check formulas, numbers, and physical claims separately

The full research page separates published comparisons, conditional derivations, and open bridges. A failed test should update the claim it constrains and its dependencies; it does not automatically invalidate every independent result.
