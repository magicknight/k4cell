K4 / 01

Start with one irreducible object.

Four points, each joined to every other, form the complete graph K₄ with a three-state quantum switch at each site (3⁴ = 81 basis states). With only three colours on four connected vertices, no classical state avoids a collision; the true quantum ground level resolves into a ninefold frustrated superposition (9 = 3 × 3). The real discipline is separating closed finite-graph theorems from open physical bridges.
