K4 scientific claims are evaluated through papers, professional criticism,
independent derivation or reproduction, Founder-signed preregistered predictions,
and comparisons with experiments or observations. Paper publication and peer
review provide professional scrutiny; they are not automatic confirmation.

Public participation has no direct weight in a scientific verdict. Public
comprehension tests whether the explanation works. Error reports and technical
contributions are welcome, but they enter the scientific evidence chain only
after satisfying the relevant mathematical, computational or experimental
acceptance condition.

The public K4 Cell surface is primarily for:

- explaining papers, predictions, comparisons and open bridges;
- finding patrons, grantmakers and sponsors who wish to support theoretical
  physics exploration;
- measuring separately disclosed speculative interest in a possible future
  pure-meme K4V.

Patron or donation support, grants or sponsorship, and speculative demand are
different channels and must never be combined into one number. Traffic,
funding, media attention and token interest do not establish K4 science.

scientific-validation requirements. The Prediction Registry foundation is now
published at [`/predictions/`](../predictions/): strict schemas, validator,
tests and exactly eleven frozen public-ledger rows classified `RETROSPECTIVE`.

inventory and the first source-native candidate remain open.
