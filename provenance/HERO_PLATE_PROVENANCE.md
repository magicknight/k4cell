# Hero plate and social cards — provenance

The first screen carries a large image. It is **concept art**: a toy simulation
rendered on the author's machine. It is not an observation, and no quantity from
the K4 Cell Framework enters it at any point.

## What the image is

A Zel'dovich (1970) first-order displacement of a uniform Lagrangian grid, seeded
by a Gaussian random field with a scale-free spectrum and a Gaussian small-scale
cut, cloud-in-cell deposited and tone-mapped through the site's own colour
tokens. This is the textbook toy that produces the filament / node / void
morphology of large-scale structure. No telescope data is used. Nothing in it is
fitted to anything.

## Why it is not regenerated by the build

The generator is deterministic for a fixed seed on a fixed toolchain, but the
WebP encoder is not guaranteed stable across Pillow versions. Running it inside
`npm run build` would churn `SITE_SHA256SUMS.txt` on every machine and destroy
the manifest's value as an integrity signal. The committed binaries are the
artefact of record; a change of seed is a reviewable diff.

The generator prints a WCAG contrast gate over the composited scrim and exits
non-zero if any text colour used over the plate falls below AA. It must be run
again, and the gate must pass, after any change to `.hero-band::after` in
`src/assets/site.css` — the CSS scrim and the generator's `alpha()` ramp are a
matched pair.

## Social cards

`tools/og_cards.py` composes the same plate with the headline and the identical
three-clause concept-art label, plus a footer strip carrying the zero-parameter
count, the best and worst rows, and the review status. Social previews are the
highest-velocity surface for the misreading this label exists to prevent, so the
label ships on the cards as well as on the page.

```text
og-k4cell-en.jpg  1200x630
og-k4cell-zh.jpg  1200x630
```

Regenerate by hand with `python3 tools/og_cards.py`; commit the result.
