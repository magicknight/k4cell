# K4 communication and support roadmap v1

> Adopted: 2026-08-31 UTC
>
> State: `ACTIVE PRIMARY ROUTE / SUPPORT CONTACT OPEN / TRANSACTIONS NOT STARTED`

## Mission card

`TARGET`

Turn K4 from a monograph-scale independent research object into a sustained,
high-impact theoretical-physics programme with public visibility, continuing
papers, preregistered experimental contact and durable funding.

`TARGET_QUANTIFIER`

The route is judged at programme scale, not by visits or applause. It advances
when it achieves one or more necessary programme nodes: a new paper, a
Founder-signed prediction registered before its target data, an independent
professional derivation or critique that changes the work, a transparent
funding relationship, or a durable institutional home.

`ACCEPTANCE`

- Communication makes the central idea memorable without disguising the state
  of the evidence.
- Papers and predictions remain the scientific route; public attention and
  capital have zero direct weight in a scientific verdict.
- The first funding relationship has written scope, disclosure, deliverables
  and no implied token, equity or scientific-control right unless separately
  and explicitly contracted under an authorised structure.
- No K4V launch occurs without a new Founder-signed legal, governance,
  security and operational gate.

`SHORTEST_CHAIN`

```text
one unforgettable public object
  -> sustained communication + papers
  -> qualified support conversations
  -> first transparent research capital
  -> preregistered predictions + future experiment comparison
  -> durable research institution
  -> separately gated K4V decision, if ever justified
```

`CURRENT_NODE`

The bilingual site, Prediction Registry foundation, Founder identity and
contact-only support surface are public. The next node is qualified outreach
and the first transparent funding relationship while the paper and prediction
pipeline continues.

`CHAMPION`

Lead with the discovery: one four-point object attempting to turn unexplained
inputs of fundamental physics into consequences of geometry. Use the numerical
comparisons, Lean corpus and experimental bets as proof of work and sources of
future scientific contact.

`ALTERNATIVE`

If direct patronage or strategic investment does not materialise, pursue
foundation grants, hosted fellowships, institutional sponsorship and research
collaborations. This alternative does not depend on token demand.

`PROBE`

Run direct, founder-led outreach using the support page: a small set of
high-fit patrons, foundations, institutes and frontier-technology partners;
record which narrative opens a substantive conversation without treating
response rate as scientific evidence.

`CORE`

The public-review monograph, two papers under review, the finite object, the
Lean corpus, the eleven retrospective comparison rows, the empty signed-ready
Prediction Registry, and the Founder-signed no-mint boundary.

`FLOORS`

No fabricated certainty, no hidden edit to frozen evidence, no payment or
wallet collection before authorisation, no promised return, no token allocation
from contact, and no conflation of funding with scientific validation.

## Roadmap

### 1. Narrative and reach — active

- Keep the homepage discovery-first and bilingual.
- Publish a repeatable visual and written story around the four-point object.
- Route serious readers to the papers, Prediction Registry and support page.
- Prepare founder-led outreach materials for patrons, foundations, research
  institutes, science media and strategically aligned investors.

### 2. First research capital — open

- Start with contact-only conversations.
- Convert a qualified conversation into a written sponsorship, patronage,
  grant, fellowship or strategic-funding agreement.
- Disclose scope, conflicts, publication policy and what the funding does not
  purchase.
- Use the runway for research time, computation, publication and professional
  review.

### 3. Prediction contact — open

- Derive the first source-native observable whose target data are not yet public.
- Freeze prediction bytes, knowledge cutoff, nuisance freedom, analysis code,
  likelihood or covariance, statistic, multiplicity policy and falsifier.
- Sign and publish it before the target release, then report the comparison
  without changing the frozen object.

### 4. Durable institution — open

- Select a vehicle capable of supporting multi-year work: hosted fellowship,
  foundation, lab, sponsored programme or equivalent.
- Separate research governance, funding governance and any later market
  governance.
- Expand professional collaboration and independent review without outsourcing
  the programme’s scientific agenda to public votes.

### 5. K4V — separate future gate

K4V remains unlaunched. A future consideration requires a new, explicit and
Founder-signed package covering legal structure, jurisdiction, governance,
security, treasury, conflicts, disclosures, launch authority and operational
readiness. Attention or speculative interest alone does not open this gate.
